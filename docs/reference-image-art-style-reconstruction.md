## Reconstructed Style Name

```txt
Neon Learning Poster Collage
```

Secondary labels:

- Hyper-neon academy poster
- Futuristic classroom collage
- Motivational learning mural
- Holographic edu-lab aesthetic
- Energy-burst subject world art

This style is more vivid and illustrative than a normal cyberpunk UI. It combines neon cyberpunk lighting with school-poster composition, 3D objects, motivational typography, and educational visual clutter.

## What The Images Have In Common

The images share a consistent visual formula:

1. One large central subject.
   A brain, bowl of food, vegetables, student/athlete, running silhouette, or wearable device sits at the center.

2. Explosive radial energy.
   Bright beams shoot outward from behind the central subject. The background feels like motion, discovery, power, or unlocked potential.

3. Saturated neon palette.
   Cyan, magenta, lime, yellow, orange, teal, and electric blue are all used heavily.

4. Mixed physical and digital worlds.
   Real objects sit in the foreground while holographic labels, dashboards, charts, icons, diagrams, checklists, or glowing boards float behind them.

5. Motivational poster typography.
   Large all-caps phrases such as "THINK SMART", "HEALTHY DIET", "ENERGY", "CONSISTENCY", or "AI" dominate the composition.

6. Layered educational props.
   Books, pencils, calendars, charts, lights, plants, food, desks, digital panels, and diagrams are arranged like a classroom/lab set.

7. Glossy 3D depth.
   Objects are rendered with high contrast, specular highlights, strong shadows, and cinematic lighting.

8. Controlled chaos.
   The images are busy and energetic, but the center subject stays obvious.

## Art Direction Summary

The style should feel like:

```txt
A futuristic learning lab poster where knowledge explodes outward as light, color, objects, diagrams, and motivational signals.
```

It should not feel like:

- flat vector illustration
- minimal SaaS UI
- plain LMS cards
- generic stock photos
- dark empty cyberpunk backgrounds
- realistic classroom photography

The style should be maximal, colorful, energetic, and memorable.

### Palette Behavior

Use color in beams, labels, object highlights, UI outlines, and progress effects.

Do not use one dominant hue for a whole page. The reference images succeed because they use multiple neon accents at once.

## Lighting

Lighting rules:

- Put a bright glow behind the main subject.
- Use radial beams or streaks to create movement.
- Use cyan and magenta rim lighting on objects.
- Let foreground objects feel glossy and dimensional.
- Use small sparkles, light particles, and glow dust sparingly around key moments.
- Keep the main subject readable even when the background is busy.

Avoid:

- flat lighting
- muddy shadows
- dark subjects without rim light
- glow that hides the educational object

## Composition Formula

Use this composition pattern for generated lesson art, subject headers, and hero visuals:

```txt
Top: Large motivational subject title or world label
Center: One large object/person/model
Behind center: Neon radial burst, chalkboard, holographic panel, or subject mural
Around center: Floating icons, labels, diagrams, checklists, data cards
Bottom: Tactile props or tools related to the lesson
```

Examples:

- Math lesson: giant fraction model or puzzle machine in center, neon number lines and graph panels behind it, counters and notebooks at bottom.
- ELA lesson: glowing story map in center, floating character/evidence cards behind it, book and pencil props at bottom.
- Science lesson: large 3D cell, ecosystem, circuit, or lab object in center, labeled diagrams and data panels behind it.
- Social studies lesson: artifact or map in center, timeline streaks and source cards behind it.
- Coding lesson: glowing logic board in center, flowcharts and debugging panels behind it.
- Health lesson: food, body system, or movement figure in center, habit tracker/checklist panels behind it.

## Typography

Typography in the reference images is loud and poster-like.

Use for art:

- Large uppercase display type.
- Thick block lettering.
- Slight tilt or perspective.
- Bright rim light or glow.
- Short phrases only.

Use for app UI:

- Keep long text readable with the body font.
- Use poster typography only for headings, level badges, subject worlds, rewards, and lesson hooks.
- Do not place long lesson explanations in all caps.

Recommended headline treatments:

- `MATH LAB`
- `THINK SMART`
- `POWER UP`
- `DISCOVERY MODE`
- `STORY STUDIO`
- `UNLOCK LEVEL 4`
- `CONSISTENCY IS KEY`
- `BUILD THE MODEL`

## UI Translation

The app should use the style in controlled layers:

### Student Home

Use:

- dark neon background
- glowing academy title
- level core with energy glow
- subject-world cards as poster panels
- radial light accents behind the current lesson
- XP bar as a neon energy strip
- reward as a glowing unlock card

Avoid:

- plain white dashboard
- tiny data cards as the main experience
- unstyled tables

### Subject World Cards

Each subject card should feel like a mini poster:

- strong neon border
- large world name
- central icon/object
- subject-specific glow color
- progress/XP as energy
- next lesson as a mission card

### Lesson Player

Each lesson should open with:

- a big poster-style hook visual
- one central educational object
- short headline
- neon diagram callouts
- tactile props or concrete model
- "what do you notice?" prompt

Then the UI should calm down for reading, practice, and tutor input.

### Tutor Help

Tutor visuals should look like holographic repair panels:

- stuck-point scanner
- diagnosis chips
- diagram mode selector
- misconception repair contrast
- first-step spotlight

### Parent/Teacher/Admin

Adult pages should borrow the brand but reduce intensity:

- dark or light professional panels
- smaller neon accents
- clear tables and status chips
- no dense poster clutter behind operational controls

## Image Prompt Template

Use this template for the Visual Learning Agent or OpenAI image generation:

```txt
Create a square educational poster illustration in a hyper-neon futuristic learning lab style.
Subject world: [WORLD NAME].
Lesson topic: [TOPIC].
Central object: [ONE LARGE EDUCATIONAL OBJECT].
Show the concept through [DIAGRAM / MODEL / SCENE].
Composition: large central subject, explosive radial neon beams behind it, floating holographic labels and diagrams around it, tactile classroom or lab props at the bottom.
Palette: electric cyan, neon magenta, laser lime, energy yellow, hot coral, deep teal, midnight navy.
Lighting: cinematic glow, strong rim light, glossy 3D objects, energetic particles, high contrast.
Typography: short readable poster headline only: "[SHORT HEADLINE]".
Learning purpose: the student should notice [WHAT TO NOTICE] and then [ACTION].
Safety: no real child faces, no copyrighted characters, no private data, no scary imagery, no clutter that hides the main concept.
Accessibility: keep the main object clear, labels short, strong contrast, no tiny essential text.
```

## Negative Prompt / Avoid List

Avoid:

- flat vector art
- beige classroom worksheet style
- muted corporate SaaS colors
- generic stock photo
- excessive unreadable text
- real child faces
- copyrighted characters
- gore, fear, weapons, adult themes
- tiny labels that are needed to understand the image
- over-dark scenes with no educational object
- visuals that are decorative but do not teach

## CSS/UI Direction

The current Neon Academy CSS should move closer to the reference images by adding:

- stronger radial burst panels
- more cyan/magenta/lime/yellow mixed accents
- poster-like hero blocks for lessons
- holographic subject cards
- light streak dividers
- image slots for subject-world art
- glowing XP and reward unlock effects

The theme should be colorful and energetic, not only dark.

## Production Use Rules

Reference-image style can guide generated assets and UI, but student-facing images still require review.

Rules:

- Every generated image must have an educational purpose.
- Images used in lessons must include alt text.
- Generated images stay in review until approved.
- Poster text inside images must be short and checked for spelling.
- The app UI must not rely on image text for essential instructions.
- Adult dashboards should not use busy poster backgrounds behind data tables.

## Acceptance Criteria

An image or screen matches this style if:

- It has one obvious central educational object.
- It uses explosive neon light or radial energy.
- It combines physical learning props with digital/holographic overlays.
- It uses multiple saturated colors, not one monotone glow.
- It feels motivational and high-energy.
- It still supports learning, not just decoration.
- Text remains readable.
- The learner can tell what to notice or do next.

## One-Line Creative Brief

Build the app like a futuristic learning lab where every subject world looks like a high-energy neon poster and every lesson turns knowledge into something students can see, touch, explain, and unlock.
