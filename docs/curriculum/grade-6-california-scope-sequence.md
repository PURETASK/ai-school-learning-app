## Math Course: Grade 6 Mathematics

Course mission: Students learn to reason with ratios, rational numbers, variables, geometry, and data so they can explain mathematical relationships, not just calculate answers.

### Unit M1: Number Systems And Arithmetic Power Tools

Core standards families: number system fluency, factors, multiples, divisibility, rational numbers, decimal operations.

Student-facing mission: Build the toolkit that lets you break numbers apart, compare them, and choose efficient strategies.

Lesson families:

- Concept model: prime factorization, factors, multiples, greatest common factor, least common multiple.
- Procedure-with-why: decimal operations and multi-digit division.
- Error clinic: common arithmetic traps, estimation checks, and reasonableness.
- Transfer lab: choose the best operation strategy in real-world constraints.

Visuals and widgets:

- Factor trees and factor-grid tiles.
- Number-line comparison for rational numbers.
- Decimal place-value slider.
- Division area-model widget.

Common misunderstandings:

- Treating factors and multiples as interchangeable.
- Moving decimal points without understanding place value.
- Believing a longer decimal is always larger.
- Skipping estimation and accepting unreasonable answers.

### Unit M2: Ratios, Rates, And Proportional Reasoning

Core standards families: ratios, equivalent ratios, unit rates, percent, tables, graphs, real-world proportional relationships.

Student-facing mission: Decode recipes, prices, maps, games, speed, and scale by comparing quantities.

Lesson families:

- Visual model: ratio tables, double number lines, tape diagrams.
- Interactive task: compare better buys and speed scenarios.
- First-principles lesson: what must stay the same in an equivalent ratio.
- Group mission: design a class event budget with rates and percent constraints.

Visuals and widgets:

- Double number-line builder.
- Ratio table generator.
- Unit-rate comparison cards.
- Percent strip and hundred-grid model.

Common misunderstandings:

- Adding the same amount instead of multiplying by the same scale factor.
- Confusing part-to-part and part-to-whole ratios.
- Treating percent as a whole number instead of "per 100."
- Choosing a cheaper total price instead of a lower unit rate.

### Unit M3: Expressions, Equations, And Variables

Core standards families: expressions, equivalent expressions, distributive property, one-variable equations, inequalities, dependent and independent variables.

Student-facing mission: Turn patterns and situations into symbols you can test, explain, and solve.

Lesson families:

- Pattern investigation: variables as changing quantities.
- Model-to-symbol: algebra tiles and area models.
- Error clinic: equal sign meaning, inverse operations, and expression versus equation.
- Project: create a rule machine that classmates must reverse-engineer.

Visuals and widgets:

- Algebra tile workspace.
- Balance-scale equation solver.
- Function table and graph connector.
- Inequality number-line tool.

Common misunderstandings:

- Seeing a variable as a mystery object instead of a quantity.
- Combining unlike terms because symbols look similar.
- Treating the equal sign as "the answer comes next."
- Forgetting that inequality direction depends on the relationship being described.

### Unit M4: Geometry, Area, Surface Area, And Volume

Core standards families: area of polygons, coordinate plane, nets, surface area, volume of rectangular prisms and prisms.

Student-facing mission: Use shape structure to measure, build, and predict space.

Lesson families:

- Visual proof: area formulas from decomposing shapes.
- Builder lab: nets to 3D solids.
- Coordinate mission: locate, reflect, and reason on the plane.
- Engineering challenge: package design under material constraints.

Visuals and widgets:

- Drag-to-decompose polygon area tool.
- Net folding simulator.
- Coordinate-plane point plotter.
- Surface area and volume comparison model.

Common misunderstandings:

- Memorizing formulas without knowing what dimensions mean.
- Confusing surface area and volume.
- Counting grid points instead of square units.
- Missing negative direction on the coordinate plane.

### Unit M5: Statistics, Data, And Variability

Core standards families: statistical questions, dot plots, histograms, box plots, measures of center, variability, mean absolute deviation, interquartile range.

Student-facing mission: Learn what data can tell you and what it cannot prove.

Lesson families:

- Data detective: distinguish statistical and non-statistical questions.
- Visual model: build and compare dot plots, histograms, and box plots.
- Reasoning lab: mean, median, range, IQR, and MAD.
- Transfer project: collect, display, and defend a class data claim.

Visuals and widgets:

- Data-set manipulator showing how one value changes mean and median.
- Dot plot and histogram builder.
- Box plot construction tool.
- Claim-evidence-reasoning data card.

Common misunderstandings:

- Treating mean as always "the typical value."
- Ignoring spread when comparing groups.
- Confusing frequency with data value.
- Making claims that the sample cannot support.

## ELA / English Course: Grade 6 Reading, Writing, Speaking, And Language

Course mission: Students learn to read closely, support claims with evidence, write clear arguments and explanations, and discuss ideas with precision.

### Unit E1: Close Reading And Evidence

Students learn to identify what the text says, what can be inferred, and what evidence supports an answer.

Core experiences:

- Annotation mission with color-coded evidence.
- Claim-evidence-reasoning builder.
- Tutor prompt: "Which sentence made you think that?"
- Partner discussion with accountable talk stems.

Common misunderstandings:

- Giving an opinion without text evidence.
- Quoting a sentence without explaining how it proves the claim.
- Confusing inference with guessing.

### Unit E2: Theme, Central Idea, And Summary

Students compare literary theme and informational central idea, then write summaries without personal opinion.

Core experiences:

- Theme-versus-topic sorting.
- Central-idea map.
- Summary compression challenge.
- Misleading-summary error clinic.

### Unit E3: Vocabulary, Word Parts, And Academic Language

Students use context, roots, affixes, reference tools, and precise word choice.

Core experiences:

- Word-part lab.
- Context-clue detective.
- Academic-language upgrade challenge.
- Visual vocabulary cards.

### Unit E4: Argument, Informative, And Narrative Writing

Students write with structure, evidence, explanation, transitions, and revision.

Core experiences:

- Claim-reason-evidence planner.
- Paragraph construction widget.
- Peer review with a rubric.
- Revision loop that asks students to strengthen evidence, organization, and language.

### Unit E5: Research, Speaking, Listening, And Presentation

Students gather sources, judge relevance, paraphrase, cite, discuss, and present.

Core experiences:

- Source reliability sort.
- Note card and paraphrase practice.
- Small-group seminar.
- Presentation studio with speaking goals.

## Science Course: Grade 6 Integrated Science

Course mission: Students explain phenomena using models, data, systems, cause and effect, scale, and engineering decisions.

### Unit S1: Earth Systems, Weather, And Climate

Anchor exemplar: `g6-earth-systems-weather`.

Core experiences:

- Weather-map investigation.
- Air-mass and pressure model.
- Data pattern analysis.
- Transfer task: predict how a different region's weather pattern changes.

Common misunderstandings:

- Weather and climate are treated as the same thing.
- Students list weather facts without explaining interactions among systems.
- Students read one data point instead of a pattern.

### Unit S2: Matter, Particles, And Energy

Students explain matter changes and energy transfer with particle models and evidence.

Core experiences:

- Particle model diagrams.
- Temperature and phase-change data lab.
- Model revision after new evidence.
- Engineering design: keep something warm or cool under constraints.

### Unit S3: Ecosystems, Food Webs, And Resources

Students analyze how organisms, energy, matter, and human decisions interact.

Core experiences:

- Food-web simulator.
- Population-change cause cards.
- Evidence debate on ecosystem impact.
- Group restoration plan.

### Unit S4: Forces, Motion, And Engineering Design

Students use data and models to explain motion, forces, and design tradeoffs.

Core experiences:

- Motion graph explorer.
- Force diagram builder.
- Prototype-test-revise cycle.
- Transfer task: explain a design failure and improvement.

## Social Studies Course: Grade 6 Ancient World History And Geography

Course mission: Students investigate ancient civilizations through geography, evidence, culture, government, economics, and historical reasoning.

### Unit H1: Historical Thinking, Geography, And Early Humans

Students learn how historians use artifacts, maps, timelines, and evidence to explain the past.

Core experiences:

- Artifact mystery lab.
- Map layers for movement and settlement.
- Timeline scale challenge.
- Source reliability discussion.

### Unit H2: Mesopotamia, Egypt, Kush, And The Fertile Crescent

Students connect rivers, agriculture, writing, law, religion, government, and social structure.

Core experiences:

- River-valley simulation.
- Code/law comparison.
- Cuneiform and writing systems artifact lab.
- Group museum exhibit.

### Unit H3: Ancient India And China

Students study geography, belief systems, innovations, government, trade, and cultural continuity.

Core experiences:

- Monsoon and river geography map lab.
- Philosophy and social order comparison.
- Silk Road trade simulation.
- Innovation impact debate.

### Unit H4: Ancient Greece And Rome

Students compare city-states, democracy, republic, empire, citizenship, law, culture, and legacy.

Core experiences:

- Polis comparison game.
- Government structure model.
- Republic-to-empire cause chain.
- Legacy argument: which idea still shapes modern life?

### Unit H5: World Regions And Later Cross-Cultural Connections

Students use geography and evidence to compare regions, trade, belief systems, and cultural exchange.

Core experiences:

- Region profile map lab.
- Trade network builder.
- Cultural diffusion case study.
- Capstone: ancient-world systems museum.
