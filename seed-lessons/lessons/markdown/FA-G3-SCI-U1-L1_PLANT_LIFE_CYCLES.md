## 2. Learning Objective and Essential Question

- **Learning Objective:** Students will sequence the major stages of a flowering plant life cycle and explain how each stage helps the plant survive and reproduce.
- **Essential Question:** How does a plant change as it grows, and why do the stages repeat?
- **Development Notes:** Third graders benefit from diagrams, sequencing cards, and concrete examples. Emphasize cycle as repeating stages, not a one-time line.

## 4. Vocabulary and Prerequisites

### Vocabulary
- **life cycle:** the stages a living thing goes through as it grows and reproduces
- **seed:** the starting structure that can grow into a new plant
- **germination:** when a seed begins to grow
- **seedling:** a young plant
- **pollination:** when pollen moves to help make seeds
- **reproduce:** to make more living things of the same kind

### Prerequisites
- Know plants are living things
- Recognize roots, stems, leaves, and flowers
- Sequence events using first/next/then/finally

## 6. Lesson Flow


### Hook (3–5 min)

Students see scrambled cards: flower, seed, seedling, sprout. They arrange them and explain why a cycle might not have a true ending.

**Student Action:** Predict, notice, or explain initial thinking before direct instruction.

**Teacher/App Action:** Capture initial response as non-graded diagnostic evidence.


### Learning Goal (1 min)

I can students will sequence the major stages of a flowering plant life cycle and explain how each stage helps the plant survive and reproduce.


### Mini Teach (6–8 min)

A plant life cycle shows how a plant grows and makes new plants. Many flowering plants begin as seeds, germinate, grow into seedlings, become mature plants, make flowers, form seeds, and begin the cycle again.


### Worked Example (5–7 min)

A bean seed gets water and warmth. It germinates, sends out a root, grows a stem and leaves, becomes a mature plant, makes flowers, and forms new seeds. The new seeds can grow into new bean plants.

**Teacher/App Action:** Highlight each reasoning step and connect it to the first-principles breakdown.


### Critical Thinking Checkpoint (3–5 min)

**Prompt:** If a diagram stops at the flower stage, what important part of the cycle is missing? Why does that matter?

**Look For:** Student identifies assumption, evaluates reasoning, and explains why one answer or model is stronger.


### Evidence-Based Reasoning Task (5–7 min)

**Prompt:** Claim: A plant life cycle repeats. Use the seed stage and seed-making stage as evidence.

**Required Structure:** Claim → Evidence → Reasoning


### Interpretation / Discussion Task (5–7 min)

**Prompt:** Interpret a diagram that uses arrows in a circle. What do the arrows mean?

**Safety:** Use private response or moderated classroom mode only. No unrestricted student chat.


### Active Practice (8–10 min)

Students drag life-cycle cards into a circular model, then click each stage to choose its function.

**Data Captured:**
- completion
- attempts
- hint usage
- mistake type
- confidence


### Reflection (2–3 min)

**Prompt:** What strategy helped you most, what mistake should you watch for, and when will you review this skill again?


## 7. First-Principles Breakdown

- **Core Concept:** A life cycle is a repeating pattern of growth and reproduction.

### Smallest Parts
- seed
- germination
- seedling
- mature plant
- flower
- new seeds
- environmental needs

### Known Facts
- Seeds can grow into plants.
- Plants need water, light, air, and space.
- Mature plants can make new seeds.

### Unknowns
- Which stage comes next in a specific example.

### Rules / Patterns
- Stages happen in an order.
- Cycles repeat when new seeds grow.
- Each stage has a function.

### Assumptions to Avoid
- Do not treat the cycle as only a straight line.
- Do not skip the seed-making stage.
- Do not assume all plants look exactly the same at every stage.

### Step-by-Step Rebuild
- Identify the current stage.
- Describe what the plant is doing in that stage.
- Name what comes next.
- Explain how the stage helps survival or reproduction.
- Connect the last stage back to seeds.

### Answer Check
- If the final stage creates seeds, the cycle can start again.

## 8. Guided Practice
- Put seed, germination, seedling, mature plant, flower, seed in order.
- Match each stage to its job.
- Explain why a mature plant is important to the cycle.
- Use evidence from a diagram to decide which stage is shown.

## 10. Quiz and Answer Key

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q01 [mc | easy]
- **Question:** Which stage usually comes first in a flowering plant life cycle? Choices: flower; seed; mature plant; fruit
- **Correct Answer:** seed
- **Explanation:** Many flowering plants begin as seeds.
- **Skill Tag:** SCI.SEQUENCE

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q02 [mc | easy]
- **Question:** What is germination? Choices: a plant making fruit; a seed beginning to grow; a flower losing petals; a leaf changing color
- **Correct Answer:** a seed beginning to grow
- **Explanation:** Germination is the start of growth from a seed.
- **Skill Tag:** SCI.VOCAB

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q03 [short | medium]
- **Question:** Name two things many seeds need to germinate.
- **Correct Answer:** water and warmth, or water and air depending context
- **Explanation:** Seeds need proper conditions to begin growing.
- **Skill Tag:** SCI.NEEDS

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q04 [mc | easy]
- **Question:** Why is a life cycle shown as a circle? Choices: Plants are round; The stages can repeat; The plant never changes; The seed is a circle
- **Correct Answer:** The stages can repeat
- **Explanation:** New seeds can start the cycle again.
- **Skill Tag:** SYSTEMS.CYCLE

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q05 [error | medium]
- **Question:** A student orders the stages: seed → flower → seedling. What is wrong?
- **Correct Answer:** A seedling grows before the mature plant makes flowers.
- **Explanation:** The sequence is incorrect.
- **Skill Tag:** MISTAKE.SEQUENCE

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q06 [explain | medium]
- **Question:** How does the flower stage help the plant life cycle continue?
- **Correct Answer:** Flowers can help produce seeds through pollination/reproduction.
- **Explanation:** Look for seed-making connection.
- **Skill Tag:** EVIDENCE.REASONING

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q07 [mc | easy]
- **Question:** Which is a young plant? Choices: seed; seedling; pollen; soil
- **Correct Answer:** seedling
- **Explanation:** A seedling is a young plant.
- **Skill Tag:** SCI.VOCAB

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q08 [apply | medium]
- **Question:** A plant has roots, stem, leaves, and flowers. What stage is it likely in?
- **Correct Answer:** Mature plant/flowering stage.
- **Explanation:** The presence of flowers suggests maturity.
- **Skill Tag:** SCI.INTERPRET_MODEL

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q09 [mc | medium]
- **Question:** Which detail is evidence that a plant is reproducing? Choices: It has green leaves; It is making new seeds; It is in soil; It is near sunlight
- **Correct Answer:** It is making new seeds
- **Explanation:** Reproduction makes new plants possible.
- **Skill Tag:** EVIDENCE.SCIENCE

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q10 [short | easy]
- **Question:** What stage comes after a seed begins to sprout?
- **Correct Answer:** seedling
- **Explanation:** After germination, it becomes a young plant.
- **Skill Tag:** SCI.SEQUENCE

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q11 [explain | hard]
- **Question:** Why would a plant without water have trouble completing its life cycle?
- **Correct Answer:** It may not germinate or grow, so it cannot mature and make seeds.
- **Explanation:** Connect needs to stages.
- **Skill Tag:** SYSTEMS.CAUSE_EFFECT

### FA-G3-SCI-U1-L1-Q12 [apply | medium]
- **Question:** Draw or describe a circular plant life-cycle model.
- **Correct Answer:** Seed → germination → seedling → mature plant/flower → seeds → seed.
- **Explanation:** Must include repeating cycle.
- **Skill Tag:** CREATE.MODEL

## 13. Reteach Path

- **Misconception:** Student treats the life cycle as a straight line and skips reproduction/new seeds.
- **Simple Explanation:** A cycle repeats. New seeds are what let the pattern begin again.

### Reteach Steps
1. Lay out stage cards in order.
2. Add arrows from one stage to the next.
3. Circle back from new seeds to seed.
4. Say the job of each stage.
5. Explain what would happen if one stage were missing.

### Reteach Practice

- First stage? → **Seed**
- Seed begins to grow = ? → **Germination**
- Young plant = ? → **Seedling**
- What lets the cycle repeat? → **New seeds**
- Fix: seed → flower → seedling → **seed → germination → seedling → mature plant/flower**

- **Exit Criteria:** Student completes at least 4 of 5 reteach items and explains the corrected misconception.
